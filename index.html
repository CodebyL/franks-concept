Why it lasts

The paint is the last step. The prep is the job.

  1. Protect the roomFloors and furniture covered before anything is opened.
  2. Fix what's under the paintCracks caulked, dings and nail holes spackled, rough spots sanded.
  3. Prime where it mattersPatches and bare surfaces primed so the color reads evenly.
  4. Two clean coatsSharp lines at the ceiling and trim, then a walk-through with you.
